Customer care

How can we help?

Find practical answers about sizing, delivery, returns and care. For anything else, write to us.

When will my order arrive?

Orders are typically prepared within 1–2 business days. Once dispatched, standard delivery across India takes 3–7 business days.

What is your returns policy?

You can request a return within 14 days of delivery, provided the item is unworn, unwashed and has all original tags.

How do I find my size?

Use the size guide on every product page. If you are between sizes, JAI can also help you choose based on your preferred fit.

How should I care for my pieces?

Cold gentle wash or dry clean as noted on the garment label. Air dry away from direct sunlight and iron on a low setting.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible. We can make changes before an order enters dispatch.
