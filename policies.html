Shipping & returns

We offer complimentary standard delivery within India on orders above ₹4,000. Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery for unworn pieces in original condition. Return delivery fees may apply where noted.

Size guide

Our pieces are designed with ease through the body. Product pages include garment-specific notes. For a close but relaxed fit, choose your usual size; size down for a neater silhouette.

Privacy

We only use the information you provide to process orders, offer support and—if you opt in—send Jaikri updates. We never sell your personal information.

Terms
